1. Introduction

WorkBit Ltd ("WorkBit", "we", "our", "us") operates the WorkBit platform and associated websites, mobile apps, and offer integrations (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you register for and use the Service, including when you participate in incentivized offers and reward programs.

2. Information We Collect

- Account information: name, email, phone number, date of birth, and any identity verification documents you provide.
- Wallet and transaction information: deposits, purchases, earnings, withdrawals, payment references, and transaction history.
- Offer/activity data: identifiers and metadata required by offer providers to verify offer completion (offer IDs, IP/UA, timestamps, device IDs) and postback data required by partner networks.
- Usage data: IP address, device information, browser, and activity logs to operate, secure, and improve the Service.
- Security signals: keyed, one-way IP fingerprints; masked email identifiers; sign-in failure counts; registration patterns; and the reasons/evidence used for manual fraud review. We do not use a shared IP address alone to automatically restrict or ban an account.
- Account appeals: the email address, one-time verification code state, and statement submitted when requesting a manual review of an account restriction.
- Communications: messages between you and support or admin; marketing preferences if given.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to: provide and maintain the Service; process deposits, package purchases, earnings, and withdrawals; validate offer completions with offer providers; comply with KYC/AML or publisher partner requirements; detect and prevent fraud; and send transactional notifications.

4. Offer Providers and Sharing

We may integrate with third-party offer providers and ad networks. A provider is enabled only when the relevant integration is approved, configured, and available. To operate an enabled offer integration, we may share a provider-specific account or click identifier, offer and campaign identifiers, country, timestamps, completion status, reward or transaction references, and technical data such as IP address and browser or device information, to the extent required by that integration. We use this information to attribute and validate eligibility and completion, prevent abuse, process rewards or provider reversals, and reconcile transactions. We may receive conversion, validation, or reversal information from the provider. The exact data depends on the provider and integration. Providers may also collect information directly through their offerwalls or tracking technologies under their own privacy notices. We do not sell personal data.

5. Payment and Postback Data

When you submit deposit or payout information (for example, mobile-banking numbers, payment references), we store the minimum data necessary to process, verify, and audit the transaction. Some provider integrations require us to include a postback secret and shared keys; those secrets are stored securely and used only for verification.

6. Wallet Balances and Withdrawals

Your wallet contains two balances: Deposit Balance (reserved for package purchases and renewals; not withdrawable) and Earning Balance (withdrawable subject to thresholds and admin policies). All exchange rates and snapshots are recorded at the time of approval; changing the platform exchange rate does not retroactively change previously approved credits.

We use industry-standard security practices to protect data in transit and at rest. Sensitive secrets (postback secrets, API keys, encryption keys) must be stored in environment variables and not committed to source control.
