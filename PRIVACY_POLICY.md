1. Introduction

WorkBit Ltd ("WorkBit", "we", "our", "us") operates the WorkBit platform and associated websites, mobile apps, and offer integrations (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you register for and use the Service, including when you participate in incentivized offers and reward programs.

2. Information We Collect

- Account information: name, email, phone number, date of birth, and any identity verification documents you provide.
- Wallet and transaction information: deposits, purchases, earnings, withdrawals, payment references, and transaction history.
- Offer/activity data: identifiers and metadata required by offer providers to verify offer completion (offer IDs, IP/UA, timestamps, device IDs) and postback data required by partner networks.
- Usage data: IP address, device information, browser, and activity logs to operate, secure, and improve the Service.
- Security signals: keyed, one-way IP fingerprints; masked email identifiers; sign-in failure counts; registration patterns; and the reasons/evidence used for manual fraud review. We do not use a shared IP address alone to automatically restrict or ban an account.
- Account appeals: the email address, one-time verification code state, and statement submitted when requesting a manual review of an account restriction.
- Communications: messages between you and support or admin; marketing preferences if given.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to: provide and maintain the Service; process deposits, package purchases, earnings, and withdrawals; validate offer completions with offer providers; comply with KYC/AML or publisher partner requirements; detect and prevent fraud; and send transactional notifications.

4. Offer Providers and Sharing

We integrate with third-party offer providers and ad networks (for example: Lootably, AdGEM, CPAlead, OfferwallGG, Monlix, BitLabs, AdGate, Torox, AdswedMedia, Revu, and others). A provider is enabled only when the relevant integration is configured and available. To operate an enabled offer integration, we may share a provider-specific pseudonymous account or click identifier, offer and campaign identifiers, completion status, reward and transaction information, country, and technical data needed to validate eligibility and completion. We do not sell personal data.

If AdswedMedia offers are enabled, the provider-hosted offerwall/tracking flow may receive the WorkBit account's provider-facing user ID, offer/click interaction data, and technical data such as IP address, browser/device request information, referrer, and country. AdswedMedia's integration documentation describes a callback containing a `subId` user identifier, transaction/click and offer identifiers, conversion status, reward/payout, country, and the user's IP address (`userIp`). These data are used to attribute and validate offers, investigate invalid traffic, process provider reversals, and reconcile rewards. We do not send your WorkBit password, withdrawal destination, or payment credentials as part of this offer integration. The provider may independently collect data through its site, offerwall, cookies, or tracking technologies under its own privacy policy. Review [AdswedMedia's Privacy Policy](https://adswedmedia.com/privacy-policy) and [Publisher Terms](https://adswedmedia.com/terms-and-services) before participating. Its public materials describe operations in the United States and United Kingdom and may involve international transfers; we do not control the provider's retention or independent processing.

AdswedMedia is currently listed as disabled and its provider-specific offer/callback integration is not implemented in WorkBit. This disclosure describes data flows that may occur if the integration is later approved and enabled; it is not a representation that WorkBit is currently sending data to AdswedMedia.

Participation in provider-hosted offers is optional. If you do not want a provider to receive the data needed to attribute offer activity, do not open or use that provider's offerwall. Browser privacy or cookie settings may affect offer attribution and completion verification. Contact WorkBit for privacy requests concerning data WorkBit controls; requests concerning a provider's independent processing should also be directed to that provider.

5. Payment and Postback Data

When you submit deposit or payout information (for example, mobile-banking numbers, payment references), we store the minimum data necessary to process, verify, and audit the transaction. Some provider integrations require us to include a postback secret and shared keys; those secrets are stored securely and used only for verification.

6. Wallet Balances and Withdrawals

Your wallet contains two balances: Deposit Balance (reserved for package purchases and renewals; not withdrawable) and Earning Balance (withdrawable subject to thresholds and admin policies). All exchange rates and snapshots are recorded at the time of approval; changing the platform exchange rate does not retroactively change previously approved credits.
