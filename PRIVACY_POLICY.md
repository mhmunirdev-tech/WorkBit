# Privacy Policy — WorkBit Ltd, USA

**Effective date: November 13, 2026**

> This policy describes WorkBit's current product behavior; it is not a certification of legal compliance.

## 1. Who we are

WorkBit Ltd, USA ("WorkBit", "we", "us", or "our") operates the WorkBit website and rewards service (the "Service"). WorkBit determines how information collected through its Service is used. For privacy requests, contact **workbitbd@gmail.com** or submit a request through WorkBit Support. We will verify requests as permitted or required by law.

This Policy covers information WorkBit handles. Offer providers, survey companies, advertising partners, and other third parties may handle information under their own privacy notices when you visit or use their services.

## 2. Information we collect and its sources

Depending on how you use the Service, WorkBit may collect:

- **Account identifiers and contact details:** name, email address, country, optional phone number, account ID, referral details, account status, and account creation and verification records. We receive most of this information from you.
- **Account and profile information:** password hash, authentication and verification records, optional avatar and time zone, and account settings.
- **Wallet and transaction information:** package and payment references, deposits, rewards, balances, withdrawal requests, payout method and destination, transaction history, and review or support notes. Saved mobile-banking payout destinations are encrypted for storage and masked in user-facing records.
- **Offer and provider information:** provider, offer, campaign, click, and transaction identifiers; timestamps; completion, reward, validation, and reversal status; and provider callback data.
- **Technical and usage information:** IP address and browser or device details in requests and offer activity, pages visited, and timestamps. WorkBit's first-party analytics stores a random browser identifier in local storage and records a keyed hash of that identifier and of the request IP, along with page and event data. Failed sign-in records use a masked email and keyed IP hash.
- **Support and appeal information:** the content of support tickets, messages, and account-appeal statements.

We also receive conversion and validation information from enabled offer and survey providers. WorkBit does not request date of birth or identity documents as part of ordinary account registration.

## 3. How and why we use information

WorkBit uses information to create and secure accounts; provide the wallet, package, offer, support, and withdrawal features; verify email and transactions; attribute and validate provider activity; calculate and record rewards; process provider reversals; prevent abuse; maintain analytics; communicate service and security updates; and comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

Where European data-protection law applies, WorkBit relies on the legal basis appropriate to the activity:

- **Contract:** to create and administer an account and provide features you request, including offers, rewards, support, and withdrawals.
- **Legitimate interests:** to secure accounts, detect abuse, maintain service reliability, and understand aggregate service usage, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
- **Legal obligation:** to retain or disclose records when applicable financial, tax, consumer-protection, or other law requires it.
- **Consent:** for a processing activity where applicable law requires consent. You may withdraw consent at any time for future processing based on that consent.

## 4. Offer providers, TheoremReach, and other disclosures

When an offer provider is enabled, WorkBit shares the provider-specific information needed to open or attribute an offer and verify its completion. This can include your WorkBit account ID or provider identifier, offer or campaign ID, country, click or transaction ID, timestamps, completion or reward status, and technical information such as IP address or browser/device details. Providers may also collect information directly through their own offerwalls, surveys, cookies, or tracking technologies.

For TheoremReach, WorkBit sends a WorkBit user identifier and transaction/placement and reward-currency configuration in the survey entry link. When loading its survey catalog, WorkBit also sends the account identifier, country, and request IP address to TheoremReach. WorkBit receives provider callback and reward-related metadata; survey answers are collected by TheoremReach and handled under TheoremReach's own privacy policy. TheoremReach says its surveys may collect device and usage information and responses that can include demographic or sensitive topics, and that responses may be shared with its survey customers. Participation is optional. Review [TheoremReach's Privacy Policy](https://info.theoremreach.com/privacy-policy/) before starting a survey; requests about information it controls should also be sent to TheoremReach.

WorkBit may also disclose information to service providers that host or secure the Service, deliver transactional email, store profile images, support payments or payouts, or provide advertising and analytics tools. These providers may process information only as needed for their services and under their own terms or applicable agreements. WorkBit may disclose information when required by law, to protect users and the Service, or as part of a business reorganization or transfer.

WorkBit does not sell personal information for money. Some advertising partners may receive or collect online identifiers, device/browser information, IP address, or ad interaction data when their ad placements load. Depending on the use, a disclosure for cross-context behavioral advertising may be considered "sharing" under California law. Use the rights process below to request an opt-out where applicable; you may also use controls offered by the advertising provider.

## 5. Cookies, local storage, and advertising

WorkBit uses an essential session cookie to keep you signed in. WorkBit also stores a random identifier in browser local storage for first-party page-usage analytics; the server stores a keyed hash rather than the raw visitor identifier. Offer providers and advertising partners may use their own cookies, local storage, device identifiers, or similar technologies when you use their services or view their placements. Browser controls can limit some storage, but doing so may affect sign-in, offer attribution, or provider features. Where consent is legally required for a non-essential technology, it must be obtained before that technology is used.

## 6. Retention and security

WorkBit keeps information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including to provide the Service, maintain financial and transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. First-party analytics and selected sign-in/fraud signals are retained for up to 90 days under the applicable event-processing jobs. Wallet, payment, withdrawal, and audit records may be kept longer where needed for accounting, legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes. Retention periods depend on record type and legal requirements.

WorkBit uses safeguards intended to protect information, including password hashing, keyed one-way identifiers for selected security and analytics records, and encryption for saved payout destinations. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 7. International processing

WorkBit is identified as a USA-based operator, and information may be processed in the United States and in other countries where WorkBit or its service providers operate. Where data-protection law restricts an international transfer, WorkBit will use a transfer mechanism required by that law. You may contact WorkBit for information about safeguards applicable to a particular transfer.

## 8. Your privacy rights

Rights depend on where you live and may be subject to verification, exceptions, and legal retention requirements.

### EEA, UK, and other regions with similar rights

Where GDPR or similar law applies, you may have the right to:

- receive information about how your personal data is used and request access to a copy;
- correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
- request deletion or restriction of processing in certain circumstances;
- receive certain data in a portable format;
- object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
- complain to your local data-protection supervisory authority.

WorkBit may use rules and risk signals to identify activity for review. A shared IP address alone does not automatically ban an account; account restrictions require an authorized review decision. Where a decision with significant effects is made solely by automated means, you may have rights to request human involvement or challenge it under applicable law.

### California residents

If the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, applies to WorkBit and to you, you may have the right to know/access the personal information collected, disclosed, or sold; request deletion or correction; opt out of sale or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising; limit certain uses or disclosures of sensitive personal information; and receive equal treatment for exercising your rights. These rights are subject to applicable exceptions.

The categories WorkBit may collect are identifiers and contact details; account and financial records; commercial and transaction information; internet or device activity; country or other location-related information; and inferences or risk signals used for account security. Sources include you, your browser/device and use of the Service, and enabled providers. We use and disclose these categories for the purposes and recipient categories described above. WorkBit does not sell personal information for money; certain ad-related disclosures may constitute "sharing" under California law as described above.

### Submitting a request

Submit an access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, or California opt-out request through WorkBit Support or **workbitbd@gmail.com**. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify your identity and protect your account. An authorized agent may submit a request where the law permits, subject to verification. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. If we deny a request, we will explain the reason where required. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

WorkBit does not currently provide self-service account deletion. You may request deletion through the process above. Some transaction, payment, security, or audit records may need to be retained or restricted rather than erased. Requests concerning information collected directly by an offer, survey, or advertising provider should also be sent to that provider.
